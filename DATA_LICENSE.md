# Data license and provenance

`dataset/Location1.csv` is the public Location1 wind-power benchmark used in
this work. It is included for research reproducibility under the terms of its
original public release. Users must also comply with the original source's
terms and cite the original benchmark.

`dataset/winf_fam1.csv` is **not** included. It is a proprietary wind farm
provided by China Huaneng Group and is subject to a separate data-use
agreement. Do not redistribute it.
